Bringing digital PCR precision to high-throughput molecular testing.

DPlexBio’s emulsion digital PCR (edPCR) combines simple emulsion generation, 3D fluorescence imaging, and AI-powered analysis to enable absolute nucleic acid quantitation without complex and expensive microfluidic partitioning.

The paradigm shift

Shifting the scaling problem from chips to compute.

Digital PCR provides absolute nucleic acid quantitation with exceptional precision. Conventional systems achieve it through complex and expensive microfluidics. DPlexBio takes a simpler path.

Conventional digital PCR

A different approach

Different-sized droplets are not a limitation. Polydispersity is an advantage.

Conventional digital PCR has been built around the assumption that accurate quantitation requires uniform partitions. We have demonstrated that accurate droplet digital PCR does not require monodisperse droplets—and, in fact, benefits from polydisperse droplets (expands dynamic range) — and thus does not require an expensive instrument or microfluidic chip to generate them.

Simple droplets. Sophisticated measurement.

Computational measurement

From fluorescence images to molecular counts.

DPlexBio’s emulsion digital PCR (edPCR) software identifies individual droplets in 3D, measures their volumes and fluorescence, classifies PCR-positive reactions, and calculates absolute nucleic acid concentration.
