# Company

DPlexBio is a life science instrumentation company based in Bothell, Washington. We were spun out of the University of Washington, building on years of research in droplet microfluidics and digital PCR. Our mission is to make the precision of digital PCR practical and affordable for routine research and high-throughput clinical testing.
