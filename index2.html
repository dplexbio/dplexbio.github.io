Emulsion digital PCR (edPCR)

AI-scaled
digital PCR

Bringing digital PCR precision to high-throughput molecular testing.

DPlexBio’s emulsion digital PCR (edPCR) combines simple emulsion generation, 3D fluorescence imaging, and AI-powered analysis to enable absolute nucleic acid quantitation without complex and expensive microfluidic partitioning.

The paradigm shift

Shifting the scaling problem from chips to compute.

Digital PCR provides absolute nucleic acid quantitation with exceptional precision. Conventional systems achieve it through complex and expensive microfluidics. DPlexBio takes a simpler path.

Conventional digital PCR

Precision is built into the consumable.

Microfluidic chips create highly uniform partitions before PCR.

Scaling depends on precision-manufactured physical components.

DPlexBio edPCR

Precision is built into the measurement.

Simple shaking creates variable-volume droplets that are measured by 3D imaging and AI analysis.

Scaling shifts toward imaging, software and computation.

edPCR replaces microfluidic partitioning with 3D imaging and computation.

Technology

Digital PCR, simplified.

The accuracy of digital PCR with qPCR-like simplicity and scalability. Generate droplets directly in standard-format wells, thermocycle, image in 3D, and let computational analysis determine absolute target concentration.

A different approach

Different-sized droplets aren’t a limitation. They’re an advantage.

Conventional digital PCR has been built around the assumption that accurate quantitation requires uniform partitions. We have demonstrated that accurate droplet digital PCR does not require monodisperse droplets—and, in fact, benefits from polydisperse droplets—and thus does not require an expensive instrument or microfluidic chip to generate them.

Computational measurement

From fluorescence images to molecular counts.

DPlexBio’s edPCR software identifies individual droplets in 3D, measures their volumes and fluorescence, classifies PCR-positive reactions, and calculates absolute nucleic acid concentration.

The edPCR platform

Digital PCR for routine laboratory workflows.

An integrated platform that combines automated fluorescence imaging and AI-based analysis using standard multi-well plates and simple emulsion chemistry.

The edPCR system has been beta tested and independently validated for UTI antimicrobial susceptibility testing (AST) in a CLIA laboratory.

DPlex Pulse – Programmable oscillating shaker

Droplet generation in seconds.

A brief, programmable shaking step emulsifies PCR reagents with an oil/surfactant mixture directly in multi-well plates—without microfluidic chips. Bulk emulsification produces millions of variable-volume droplets across a plate, and droplet sizing during image analysis enables absolute quantitation without requiring monodisperse partitions.

DPlex Express DX – edPCR reader

Automated 3D fluorescence imaging.

The edPCR reader uses spinning-disk confocal imaging to acquire three-dimensional fluorescence data from droplets directly in standard-format well plates.

3D fluorescence imagingAI-based droplet segmentationAutomated analysis

  • High-throughput imaging: ~25,000 droplets analyzed per minute.
  • Accurate quantitation: Droplet counting and volume measurements enable absolute nucleic acid concentration.
  • Standard thermal cyclers: Emulsions are amplified in multi-well plates before automated readout.
  • Software: converts 3D fluorescence images into absolute concentrations, with AI enhancement and TOAST adaptive acquisition for faster runs.

Reagents

Simple chemistry. Stable emulsions.

The reagent system enables PCR droplets to be generated by a brief shaking step, stabilized, thermocycled, and measured directly in the plate.
