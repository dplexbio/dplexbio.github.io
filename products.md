# Products

The edPCR system combines an imaging instrument, a droplet-generating shaker, and proprietary reagents into a complete digital PCR workflow.

## DPlex Express DX

A spinning-disk confocal microscope built for emulsion digital PCR. It scans droplets in 3D across standard well plates, supports a reference channel plus nine spectral channels, and includes analysis software that reports absolute concentration automatically.

*Details coming soon.*

## DPlex Pulse

A programmable plate shaker that turns PCR reactions into droplet emulsions in about 10 seconds per plate, with no microfluidic chips.

*Details coming soon.*

## edPCR Emulsion Oil

A crosslinking oil containing a fluorescent reference dye. It forms and stabilizes droplets for thermocycling and is index-matched for deep, clear imaging.

*Details coming soon.*

## edPCR Index Matching Aqueous Additive Mix

An additive for your PCR master mix that matches the reaction's refractive index to the emulsion oil, making the emulsion optically clear for 3D imaging. It is compatible with widely used commercial master mixes.
